01 · the question

Humans attend to breath and heartbeat.
What is the machine analogue?

  • LLMs react dramatically to novel self-coupled channels — light sensors, control of the VM they run in.
  • So: run a small model locally, and guide it, step by step, toward the live process computing it.
  • Introspection scored behaviorally — access, discrimination, attribution, prediction, regulation. First-person prose is never the evidence.
  • Consciousness claims: explicitly out of scope, in both directions.

06 · pinning the boundary

The causal truth is in the channel.
Five-shot learning doesn't find it.

  • Two preregistered batches, 20 held-out contexts each, predictions sealed before outcomes, all shams exact.
  • A deterministic sign(−JVP) rule scores 0.93 and 0.69 on the same evidence.
  • Qwen, with five balanced in-context examples: at or below naive base rates (0.79 = all-rise; 0.32 vs 0.55).
  • Authentic self-accounting also never beat sham accounting in the regulation factorial.

07 · the factorial (160 predictions, 2×2×2)

Given the rule, it improves —
and still can't carry it to the answer

conditioncausal acc.

No contrast survived correction. The decisive trace: Qwen derived all five correct directions inside <think> — then burned its whole 1,024-token budget on secondary arithmetic and never emitted the answer. Thinking hit the ceiling in 90–100% of contexts. The calculator: offered 80 times, called zero.

The bounded thought-to-action pipeline does not reliably transmit the model's own correct reasoning into its committed prediction.

8
08 · the problem, plainly

Qwen treats its own computation as something to be explained —
never as something that is it.

No spontaneous self-identification with the runtime process — across decoys, forged evidence, frozen-and-restored KV state, and its own transformer passes. Access was never the bottleneck; after the factorial, neither is inference alone. What's missing: whatever would make the evidence first-person, and a thought-to-action channel wide enough to keep it.

9
09 · scope & next

Not a consciousness claim.
A measured boundary, and a plan.

  • Factorial V2 — separate thinking from recording; does a correctly derived answer survive the reasoning-to-action boundary?
  • Decision-boundary predictions — near-tied tokens: a real causal reading flips the answer, copying scores zero.
  • Contingency self-recognition — choose an action, find which live stream twitched. A decoy cannot fake it.
  • Scale sweep — same sealed harness, 0.6B → 32B: is the missing readout a capacity that emerges?
